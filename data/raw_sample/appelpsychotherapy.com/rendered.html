    About

    Caren Ann Appel, LCSW, MA
    (she/her)

    Finding the right therapist is one of the most important decisions you will make. A bold statement, but let me tell you why: Because you are meant to build a life authentic to YOU. I work with people late-diagnosed with ADHD, autism, & other neurodivergent subtypes. Some have a diagnosis, some only suspect they may have ADHD. Both come to me because I offer guidance when their usual strategies are no longer working. Oftentimes, their career and personal relationships are being adversely affected. I'm here to tell you: it's not supposed to be this hard. I also get a lot of what I call 'mixed' couples: where one partner has ADHD, the other is neurotypical-and they've fallen into a parent-child dynamic, instead of partners who can rely on each other.
