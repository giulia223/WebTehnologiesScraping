A place with purpose

Education is the heart. The bush is the invitation.

Our flagship program is professional veterinary CE: an intensive course offered exclusively with Texas A&M and open to practicing veterinarians from any school, practice or location. Seasonal student programs and private lodge stays complete the Seringa experience.

Rooted in South Africa, Seringa combines thoughtful hospitality with the character of the Waterberg.
