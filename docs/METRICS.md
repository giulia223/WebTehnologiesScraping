# Metrics

Generated by `techdetect evaluate metrics` from `data/ground_truth.json` and the current `data/output/detections.jsonl`. Do not edit by hand.

- **Labelled domains:** 10 blind (every 20th input row, picked by position before the tool existed) and 10 review (drawn at random with a fixed seed among the domains the tool could read).
- **Precision** uses the verdict on every detection of these domains (347 detections). Each was judged correct / wrong / unsure from its evidence and the saved raw data, by reviewers separate from the tool's author, following `docs/DEFINITIONS.md` (what counts as a technology the site uses). Unsure detections are left out of the percentage and counted apart.
- **Recall** uses the blind domains only: an independent inspection listed every technology it could confirm before seeing any output of the tool. Recall is the share of that list the tool reports, in any group. It is measured against what that inspection found, so technologies it missed are missing here too: the real recall is likely lower.
- **F1** is the harmonic mean of the two.
- Every rate carries its counts. With fewer than 10 judged detections a breakdown says "too few to measure".
