# Metrics

Generated by `techdetect evaluate metrics` from `data/ground_truth.json` and the current `data/output/detections.jsonl`. Do not edit by hand.

- **Labelled domains:** 10 blind (every 20th input row, picked by position before the tool existed) and 10 review (drawn at random with a fixed seed among the domains the tool could read).
- **Precision** uses the verdict on every counted detection of these domains (332 detections; 1 more, below the default confidence threshold, is not counted). Each was judged correct / wrong / unsure from its evidence and the saved raw data, by reviewers separate from the tool's author, following `docs/DEFINITIONS.md` (what counts as a technology the site uses). Unsure detections are left out of the percentage and counted apart.
- **Recall** uses the blind domains only: an independent inspection listed every technology it could confirm before seeing any output of the tool. Recall is the share of that list the tool reports, in any group. It is measured against what that inspection found, so technologies it missed are missing here too: the real recall is likely lower. Part of it is also fitted to this set: on 2026-11-22, 9 rules were written from misses on these same blind domains. Before them recall was 66% (75/113), the last independent figure; the rules' real test is a fresh set of domains.
- **F1** is the harmonic mean of the two.
- Every rate carries its counts. With fewer than 10 judged detections a breakdown says "too few to measure".
- **Left out of precision:** 18 detections the tool reports apart as a hosted platform's infrastructure (servers, CDN, certificate, bot protection, the platform's own tracking: not the site's choice). 18 of them are labelled "not the site's" (wrong as a claim about the site), so moving them apart agrees with the labels there.
